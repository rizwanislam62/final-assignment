Ranking criteria (soft preferences)
===================================
Key          Label          Type      How it is scored (0-100, 100 = best)
-----------  -------------  --------  ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
price        Price          core      Ticket price; cheapest acceptable offer = 100 (robust min-max, lower is better).
duration     Duration       core      Journey time; fastest acceptable offer = 100 (robust min-max, lower is better).
departure    Departure fit  core      Minutes outside the preferred departure window; 100 inside the window, falling linearly to 0 at the decay limit (default 4 hours).
flexibility  Flexibility    core      Fare flexibility points: free changes 60, changes with a fee 40, no changes 0; refundable adds 40 (max 100).
transfers    Transfers      optional  Direct = 100, one transfer = 50, two or more = 0.
rating       Rating         optional  Customer rating mapped linearly from the rating scale (1-5) to 0-100.
emissions    CO2            optional  Estimated CO2 per passenger; lowest acceptable offer = 100 (robust min-max).

Default weights: price=40, duration=25, departure=15, flexibility=20
